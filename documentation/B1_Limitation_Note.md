# B1 – Identified Project Limitation

## Project
Perishable Stock Verification Register (FreshFirst Team)

## What Currently Cannot Be Proved

The system cannot independently confirm that the physical stock quantity matches the quantity recorded in the database.

## Why This Cannot Be Proved

The system depends on staff to count and enter the actual physical stock quantity. Without a physical stock count and verification, the database record alone cannot prove the quantity available in storage.

## How the Limitation Can Be Addressed

1. Staff physically count the available stock.
2. Staff compare the physical quantity with the database quantity.
3. Any mismatch is recorded and reviewed.
4. Verification details are maintained for traceability.

## Evidence

The FreshFirst MySQL database stores product quantity records. These records support stock tracking but do not independently prove physical stock availability.
